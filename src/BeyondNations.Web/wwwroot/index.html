Controls
  • WASD or arrow keys: move and turn
  • Hold the right mouse button and move the mouse: look around
  • E interact, Space jump, Shift sprint
  • N create a nation, J join one, F found a settlement
  • I inventory, O nations, diplomacy and laws, V first/third person, Esc pause

Beyond Nations

This game is played with a keyboard and mouse. On a phone or tablet it works best with a keyboard attached.

You can still try it with on-screen controls: a pad to walk, buttons for the main keys, and a drag on the world to look around.
