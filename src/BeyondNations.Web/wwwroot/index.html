Controls
  • WASD or arrow keys: move and turn
  • Hold the right mouse button and move the mouse: look around
  • E interact, Space jump, Shift sprint
  • N create a nation, J join one, F found a settlement
  • I inventory, O nations, diplomacy and laws, V first/third person, Esc pause
  • On a phone or tablet: the stick (left) walks and turns, a drag on the world steers, a tap on the world uses what is within reach, and the buttons do the rest
